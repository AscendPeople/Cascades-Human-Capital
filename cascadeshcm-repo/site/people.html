Four ways to engage the capability

01

Fractional HR Leadership

A standing monthly partnership that gives the organization executive people-leadership without the full-time hire.

Best for: organizations that need ongoing senior leadership at a sustainable cost.
  • Executive and board-level HR consultation
  • Workforce planning and organizational design guidance
  • Leadership coaching for department heads
  • A people strategy aligned to the business strategy
02

Interim Leadership

Full ownership of the function through a vacancy, search, or transition, with a documented handoff at the end.

Best for: a departed leader, a merger or restructure, or any season where the seat cannot sit empty.
  • Day-one ownership of the function
  • Stabilization of urgent employee relations and compliance matters
  • Leadership of the existing team
  • Support for the permanent search
03

Workforce Strategy & Planning

The capability, headcount, and cost the strategy actually requires, in a form a board will test.

Best for: decisions that have to survive a budget review.
  • Capability gap analysis
  • Headcount and cost scenarios
  • Organizational design: spans, layers, decision rights
  • Plan-to-actual tracking
04

HR Operations & Infrastructure

The operational core the business runs on, documented, owned, and built to scale rather than absorbed by one person.

Best for: a people function running on undocumented practice.
  • Policy and compliance posture
  • Employee relations standards
  • Process documentation and service model design
  • Benefits and leave administration

Every engagement is scoped to the cadence you need, from advisory hours to standing leadership.

Fractional HR leadership

Common questions

What is a fractional HR leader?
A senior HR executive who leads your people function part-time, on a set monthly cadence. You get CHRO-level judgment on structure, compliance, and talent without the cost of a full-time executive hire.
When does a company need a fractional CHRO?
Usually between roughly 25 and 500 employees: when HR risk outgrows an office manager or generalist, during rapid growth, a restructure, an acquisition, or while a permanent HR leader is being recruited.
What is the difference between fractional and interim HR leadership?
Fractional leadership is ongoing and part-time. Interim leadership is full ownership of the HR function for a defined period, such as covering a vacancy, and ends with a documented handoff.
Does Cascades work with companies outside the United States?
Yes. Cascades works with organizations nationally and internationally, remotely or on site.
How does an engagement start?
With a free 60-minute consult. We agree the problem, scope, and success measures in writing before any work begins.
