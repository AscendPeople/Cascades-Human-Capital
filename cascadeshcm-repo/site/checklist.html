Questions

Before you download

Who is this checklist for?
CEOs, founders, presidents, and COOs of organizations with roughly 50 to 500 employees, especially companies that have grown without a senior HR leader.
How long does it take?
About 15 minutes. You can complete it on screen or print it, and your responses save in your browser.
Is this a compliance audit?
No. It is an executive-level diagnostic of leadership, structure, operations, systems, and talent. It is not legal advice.
What happens after I download it?
You get immediate access. If you want help ranking what you find, you can book a complimentary 30-minute strategy consultation. There is no obligation.
What is fractional HR leadership?
A senior HR executive, such as a fractional CHRO, who leads your people function part-time on a set cadence, giving you executive-level HR without a full-time hire.
